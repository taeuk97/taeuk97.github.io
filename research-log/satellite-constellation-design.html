Simulation 01

Dawn–Dusk Orbit Simulation

A dawn–dusk orbit is a special type of Sun-synchronous orbit in which the orbital plane remains close to Earth's day–night terminator, with equatorial crossings near 06:00 and 18:00 local solar time. By maintaining an approximately constant local solar time, the orbit provides near-continuous solar illumination, making it particularly advantageous for spacecraft with high and sustained power demands.
